Scope: whole SmartHire app shell (app/unified/index.html): Roles, Candidates, Assessment, Dashboard. Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led.
Audience: recruiter driving the workflow on their own laptop; candidate borrows the screen for tests. Currently judged as a live college demo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every candidate is a record card in a recruiter's file drawer, and the four phases are its colour-coded index tabs. Refuses the category default of a grey SaaS dashboard with one accent, gradients and glass.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink-blue rail (fountain-pen ink) beside a cool drawer-grey ground. White record cards topped by a single thin red header rule. Index-stock phase colours with jobs: Resume blue, Aptitude canary, Coding green, Interview cherry. Libre Franklin for all UI; JetBrains Mono only for figures, timers and code. Square-ish 4px corners, hairline rules, neutral offset shadows.

STORY: The recruiter sees at once where every candidate stands, opens one card, reads why each phase scored what it did, and rates the interview themselves.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left ink rail with the one full title "SmartHire — AI Recruitment Platform", four numbered workflow steps, and a plain local-only note. Main area: page heading, then the working card (form, record or table).
